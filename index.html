About Our Organization

Brahma Kumaris Ishwariya Vishwa Vidyalaya

A spiritual movement founded in 1936, now operating in over 140 countries across all continents with general consultative status with the United Nations ECOSOC.

Our Spiritual Mission

The purpose of the Brahma Kumaris is to help people rediscover their intrinsic goodness, peace, and spiritual dignity. By teaching Raja Yoga meditation and values-based living, we empower individuals to overcome stress, cultivate positive relationships, and contribute to an atmosphere of worldwide harmony and benevolence.
